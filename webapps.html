GoWheel·las

GoWheel·las was my rental-car service project during the course. Two different ports in the node.js server make separate entry points for the employees and the customers.
The employees can add a new vehicle, or modify the vehicle information, at the same time, they also know how many of them are currently rented. The customers can see the information dynamically, check availability of the vehicle, and rent it. They can also save their personal information for repeated use.
