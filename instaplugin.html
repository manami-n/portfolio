Main Ideas

Instagram plugin was my second personal project(keeping the ownership admitted) during my internship. The idea is people can use plugin for free to show their Instagram posts in their websites.
Unfortunately, I didn't have enough time to finish the project, the Wordpress inplementation was still in process.
